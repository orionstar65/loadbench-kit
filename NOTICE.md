# Attribution and project history

Loadbench Kit is a standalone adaptation of performance-testing utilities
previously developed in the Pryon performance package. Parts of its source
and tests share that project's implementation history.

The releasing maintainer states that they are authorized to publish,
modify, and distribute this material. The original contributor record
remains relevant, and this notice should not be interpreted as proof of
a copyright assignment or as an endorsement by Pryon.

Loadbench Kit is distributed under the terms in LICENSE. Questions about
the original source's sharing permission should be directed to the
maintainer, with supporting authorization available where required.
